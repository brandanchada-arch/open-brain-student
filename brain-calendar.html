Brain Calendar

A personal planning tool, built by Brandan Chada for his own use.

What it does

  • Shows Brandan's classes and appointments from his Google Calendar next to his to-do list, in one "Today" and "This week" view.
  • Suggests times for study blocks and tasks, and adds them only after Brandan approves.
  • Puts everything it adds on a separate calendar called "Brain" in Brandan's own Google account, never on his main calendar.
  • Sends Brandan short reminders, check-ins and a morning summary through a private Telegram bot.
  • Reads the Gmail messages Brandan labels for it (school mail and job search mail), tells him about the important ones, scores job-alert openings, and keeps a job pipeline. For emails that ask to set up a time, it writes a reply draft in Gmail Drafts. It never sends email; Brandan sends every reply himself.

Who can use it

Brain Calendar is not a public service. It has one user: its owner, Brandan Chada. It only works with his own Google account and his own data. Nobody else can sign up or sign in.
